# Privacy Policy for WinTrackr

**Last updated:** September 3rd, 2026

WinTrackr is designed with a strict "Privacy by Design" approach.

### 1. Data Collection & Storage
- **100% Local Storage:** All tracking data (active window titles, process names, timestamps, and idle time) is stored exclusively on your local computer in a local SQLite database (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\WinTrackr\WinTrackr.db`).
- **No Cloud Transmission:** WinTrackr does not send your tracking data, activity history, or personal data to any external server or cloud service.
- **No Third-Party Sharing:** Your data is never sold, shared, or accessible to the developers or third parties.
 
### 2. Telemetry and Analytics
- WinTrackr contains no third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or telemetry trackers.
- Microsoft Store license checks are handled directly via the official Windows Store APIs (`Windows.Services.Store`) by Microsoft.
  
### 3. User Control & Data Deletion
- You have complete control over your data. You can inspect, edit, or delete activities, rules, and projects directly in the app at any time, or delete the local database file.
