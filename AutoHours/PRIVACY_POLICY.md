# Privacy Policy for AutoHours

**Last updated:** September 4, 2026

AutoHours is designed with a strict "Privacy by Design" principle. We believe your work activity and time logs are confidential and belong solely to you.

---

### 1. Data Collection & Local Storage
- **100% Local Storage:** All tracking data (active window titles, running application process names, timestamps, project assignments, and idle duration) is stored exclusively on your local device in a private SQLite database (%LOCALAPPDATA%\WinTrackr\WinTrackr.db).
- **No Cloud Transmission:** AutoHours does not transmit, synchronize, or upload your tracking data, logs, or personal information to any external server or cloud service.
- **No Third-Party Access:** Your data is never sold, shared, rented, or made accessible to third parties, advertising networks, or the developers.

### 2. Telemetry and Analytics
- AutoHours contains **no third-party tracking SDKs**, analytics services (such as Google Analytics or Mixpanel), or advertising frameworks.
- Microsoft Store license validation and purchase transactions are processed directly and securely by Microsoft via the standard Windows Store APIs (Windows.Services.Store).

### 3. User Data Control & Deletion
- You retain complete ownership and control over your recorded data.
- You can inspect, edit, or delete individual activities, rules, and projects directly within the app at any time.
- To permanently delete all tracking history, you can delete the local SQLite database file located in your application data folder or reset data through the app settings.

---

### Contact
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or AutoHours, please open an issue in this repository or contact support via the Microsoft Store product page.
